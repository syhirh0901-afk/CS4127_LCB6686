Attractions

Explore beautiful places across Brunei’s four districts.

DISCOVER PLACES

Where Will You Explore?

Brunei Arts and Handicraft Training Centre

Discover intricate textiles, traditional brocades, and skillfully crafted works that celebrate Brunei’s cultural legacy.

Teraja Longhouse

Beyond the edge of the rainforest lies Teraja, where one roof shelters many stories.

The Fisherman’s Hut

The Fisherman’s Hut, located along Pantai Seri Kenangan in Tutong, Brunei, offers a casual, beachside dining experience perfect for seafood enthusiasts.

Kampong Ayer

Kampong Ayer welcomes you to drift through its historic channels, walk its wooden lanes, and feel the heartbeat of a community that has flourished for over a thousand years.

Pasarneka Tutong

Pasarneka tutong is a treasure trove of bargain worthy handicrafts and local cuisine.

Brunei History Centre

Discover Brunei’s heritage through antique artifacts, intricate weaponry, and rare shipwreck treasures that bring the nation’s rich past vividly to life.

Peliunan Park

Camp, fish, ride ATVs, or share laughter over local bites in the heart of nature’s calm.

Balai Khazanah Islam Sultan Haji Hassanal Bolkiah

Explore nine galleries housing a vast array of Islamic treasures, from ancient manuscripts to sacred relics.

Jame’ ‘Asr Hassanil Bolkiah Mosque

A spectacular tribute to Brunei’s current Sultan, Jame’ ‘Asr Hassanil Bolkiah Mosque impresses with its golden domes

Malay Technology Museum

Malay Technology Museum, where traditions, tools, and lifestyles of the nation’s ethnic communities come to life.

Royal Regalia Museum

The Royal Regalia Museum is home to the Sultanate’s heirlooms and regalia.

Omar ‘Ali Saifuddien Mosque

Marvel at the grandeur of Omar ‘Ali Saifuddien Mosque, where golden domes and marble pillars meet serene lagoons in the heart of Brunei’s capital.

The Empire Brunei Country Club

The Empire Hotel & Country Club in Brunei is a prestigious luxury resort set on 445 acres of beachfront overlooking the South China Sea.

ATV Katimahar Agri Park

ATV Katimahar Agri Park offers thrilling off-road adventures through lush plantations, jungles, and a mini zoo featuring everything from ostriches to wild crocodiles.

Tasek Lama Park

Just minutes from the heart of Bandar Seri Begawan, Tasek Lama Park is an urban oasis where nature lovers can wander through lush trails, take in a serene waterfall, and discover breathtaking city views.

Jerudong Park

Brunei’s famous amusement park, and the largest in Borneo

Gadong Night Market

Brunei’s legendary night market right on the outskirts of Gadong, with some dishes being sold for as low as BND1.

Chop Jing Chew

Indulge in the ‘Roti Kuning’, a soft, buttery bread made with a recipe handed down through the generations.

Tamu Kianggeh

Possibly the best place for a quick, affordable breakfast and a casual morning stroll, absorbing the sights and sounds of a thriving market. Located just along the Kianggeh River.

Eco Ponies Garden

Experience the charm of countryside living at Eco Ponies Garden, a family-run farm in Tutong where fresh harvests transform into memorable dining experiences.

Angkasa Oud Agarwood Company

As its name suggests, Angkasa Oud Agarwood Company is a locally owned company that manufactures using – a dark fragrant resinous wood found in the forests Of Brunei.

Bintudoh Greenspring Resort

Once a thriving fruit plantation, Bintudoh Greenspring Resort now blends adventure with rustic charm.

Warisan Puk Balaban

Experience the magic of the night at Warisan Puk Balaban, Tutong’s premier stargazing destination.

Tutong River

Set off on a gentle river cruise through Tutong’s mangrove forests and glimpse wildlife in their natural rhythm

Lamin Kampung (Pangkalan Warisan)

Step inside, create, and reconnect with the cultural heartbeat of Brunei.

Tasek Merimbun Heritage Park

An ASEAN Heritage site rich in biodiversity, cultural heritage, and picturesque wetlands that offer tranquility and natural beauty.

Kiri Monument

Kuala Belait’s Kiri Monument also famously known as the Giant Teapot is one of the district’s most prominent landmarks.

Billionth Barrel Monument

Built in 1991, the Billionth Barrel Monument resides along the beach in Seria and is a symbol of achievement in Brunei’s oil production.

Seria Energy Lab

Check out the Seria Energy Lab, a centre where kids can play and learn!

Belait District Museum

Step into history at the Belait District Museum, a former British residence turned cultural landmark, where Brunei’s heritage, local wildlife, and royal memories come to life.

Ulu Temburong National Park

Immerse yourself in Brunei’s “Green Jewel,” where ancient rainforests teem with vibrant wildlife and breathtaking landscapes await.

Sumbiling Eco Village

Experience riverside glamping, local flavors, and the quiet rhythm of life at this peaceful eco retreat.

Freme Rainforest Lodge & Adventure Park

From canopy walks and river rides to fireside evenings and waterfall treks, experience Brunei’s wild side in comfort and style.

Abode River Cruise

Glide through serene mangrove forests along the Labu River and immerse yourself in Brunei’s rich biodiversity.

AZ Back to Nature

AZ Back to Nature offers customized, sustainable rainforest adventures in Brunei.

Mendaram Besar Longhouse

Experience the hospitality of the Iban indigenous community at this longhouse in Labi.
